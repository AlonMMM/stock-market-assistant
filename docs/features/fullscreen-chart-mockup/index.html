Full screen, with SPY

Every stock chart gets an expand button at its top right. It opens a dark, full-window chart that overlays the stock and SPY on their own price axes: SPY on the left, the stock on the right. Pick the bar size: 1m (yesterday and today), 5m, 15m, 1h or 1D. Point at the chart to see the crosshair and data window. Use the buttons below to open it from each place in the app.

Two price axesSPY on the left in white, the stock on the right in green, each scaled to its own day range. The lines show real prices, not %. The data window gives % from the open for both.
Crosshair and data windowThe crosshair shows the price on both axes and the time below. The two legend lines at the top and the data window show each symbol's open, high, low and close for that minute.
Keeps the contextFrom an alert, the alert window stays shaded. From the market chart, it shows QQQ vs SPY. A sector ETF can replace SPY. Candles is one tap away.
Volume vs the last monthThe top bar shows today's volume so far, how many times the 20-session average by this time it is, and its percentile. The volume pane has a dashed average line. The data window rates each bar the same way.
VWAPA yellow line on the stock's axis shows the session VWAP. It starts again at each day's open. Its tag, the legend and the data window say how far the stock is above or below it. It's off by default; press VWAP to show it on 1m to 1h.
Bar sizes1m shows yesterday and today. 5m shows 5 sessions, 15m 10 sessions, 1h 30 sessions, and 1D a year. A bold dashed line marks where each day (or month, on 1D) starts. You can drag to see more and zoom with the wheel or a pinch.
Easy to leaveClose it with ✕, Esc or Back. You return to the same row in the same place. The small chart in the row doesn't change.
